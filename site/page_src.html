Periwinkle Shell Genes

Which parts of a snail's genome go with Raup's coiling parameters? Results so far from an F2 cross of two periwinkle ecotypes (Crab and Wave), placed on the 2024 genome, checked against tissue expression, and compared across 20 molluscs.

LG17 · 64–70 Mb

Coil expansion (W) and distance from axis (D)

W is significant (LOD 4.43). D sits at the threshold. 174 genes, including dispatched genes from a mollusc-only lineage that is missing in shell-less species, though barely active in the adult mantle.

LG6 · 0–17.7 Mb inversion

Opening shape (S) and shell size

Just below the threshold (LOD 3.82). Holds the periwinkle version of the pond-snail coiling gene and 8 strongly mantle-specific genes.

Status

Candidates, not causes

Each region is millions of bases inherited as a block. Separating genes needs recombinant snails or expression at the shell edge.

1 · Shell parameters

From shell geometry to measured traits

Raup (1966) builds any coiled shell by sweeping a growing opening around an axis. Koch et al. (2021) measured 350 F2 snails with a growth model that translates into Raup's terms.

4 · The pond-snail coiling gene

LG6 holds the periwinkle version of Lsdia1

In the pond snail Lymnaea, losing the formin Lsdia1 flips the coil from right- to left-handed (Davison et al. 2016). Protein alignment shows the LG6 diaphanous gene is periwinkle's match for it.

Periwinkle proteinvs Lymnaea Lsdia1vs Lymnaea Lsdia2
diaphanous-2-like, LG6 QTL XP_070197016.165.7%69.2%
diaphanous-1-like, LG3 XP_070189802.134.8%33.7%

Lsdia1 and Lsdia2 are 87.9% identical to each other: a duplication in the Lymnaea lineage. The gene tree in section 8 confirms periwinkle has a single diaphanous gene, the LG6 one. The LG3 "diaphanous-1-like" gene is a misnamed fragment that falls outside the clade.

5 · Mantle expression

Which candidates are switched on in shell-building tissue

Gene activity (TPM) from NCBI's tissue panel: mantle ×2, foot ×2, hepatopancreas ×2, reproductive ×2, penis, head. Genes are ranked by how mantle-biased they are among 23,211 expressed genes. Known shell genes (carbonic anhydrases, tyrosinase, chitin synthase, nacrein) rank in the top 2%, so the ranking picks up shell genes.

  • Diaphanous-2-like (LG6) is active in the mantle, but about as much in foot and head. Lsdia1 acts in the early embryo, so adult tissue cannot test it.
  • The LG17 dispatched genes are close to silent in the mantle. This weakens them as shell-growth candidates, at least in adults.
6 · LG6 in detail

A 17 Mb block with no recombination

Top: genetic position (cM) against physical position (Mb). The flat run from 0 to 17.7 Mb means no crossing-over, the signature of an inversion. Bottom: the S signal along the chromosome, with the 14 genes that rank in the genome's top 5% for mantle bias.

#MbBlockGene (NCBI name)Mantle TPMOther tissues

The S peak (14.8 Mb) sits beside CD109-like and cytochrome P450 2U1-like, but an F2 cross cannot tell genes inside the block apart. The mantle also makes mucus and immune proteins, so CD109, murinoglobulin and lipoxygenase may not be shell genes.

  • Diaphanous: by name, left-coiling snails seemed to have fewer copies. The gene tree shows this was a naming artefact.

8 · Gene trees

Dispatched: a mollusc-only lineage, missing where the shell is lost

Maximum-likelihood trees (MAFFT, trimAl, IQ-TREE, 1,000 bootstraps) of all 295 formins and 75 dispatched proteins from these molluscs plus human and fruit fly. Genes are grouped by clades anchored on human and fly genes.

Dispatched groupHuman / fly memberPeriwinkle copiesAcross molluscs
DISP1/2 (releases Hedgehog)DISP1, DISP2, fly disp1exactly 1 in every species. Periwinkle's sits at LG17 70.0 Mb, in the W region
DISP3DISP310–2 per species
Mollusc-specific lineage, sister to DISP1/2none6all on LG17, 3 of them in the W region; expanded in periwinkle's group
  • The mollusc-specific lineage is in 12 of 14 species with an external shell and none of the 5 without one (octopuses, squid, Aplysia, sea slug).
  • Those 5 absences come from only 2–3 independent losses, and a missing gene can be an annotation gap, so this is a lead, not a result.
  • Periwinkle's copies are barely active in the adult mantle. A test would need the developing shell gland or juvenile mantle.
  • Diaphanous is single-copy in 13 of 19 molluscs, including periwinkle and both left-coiling snails. Copy number does not track coiling direction.
